Ayn Thor - what to convert, and to what
=======================================

Each line names a game, the ES-DE folder it lives in, and the format to
convert it to. Both shapes below are read; use whichever you prefer. Lines that
are neither are ignored, so headings and notes can sit between the entries.

Folder names follow the ES-DE layout:
https://github.com/retrogamecorps/ES-DE-Directories

Format tokens:
  CHD       disc images   (psx, ps2, psp, dreamcast, saturn, pcenginecd)
  RVZ       GameCube and Wii
  ZCCI      Nintendo 3DS  (decrypt the ROM first)
  NSZ       Nintendo Switch
  WUA       Wii U
  7Z        cartridge ROMs read by RetroArch
  ZIP       arcade romsets (FBNeo and MAME will not read 7z)
  NDS-TRIM  Nintendo DS, trim padding only
  Z64       leave uncompressed; this is the most compatible form
  -         do not convert

Notes
-----

PS2 needs zlib and hunk 2048 for NetherSX2; it cannot read a zstd CHD.
3DS ROMs must be decrypted before ZCCI compression will save anything.
Switch base games, updates and DLC are separate files and compress separately.
